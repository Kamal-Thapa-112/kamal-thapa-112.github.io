01 / DATA SCIENCEAWARD WINNING PROJECT

Understanding the harvest.

Farmer Harvest Yield & Profit Analytics

I collected crop yield, harvest timing, and profit data from farmers in Nepal to understand seasonal patterns and forecast harvests.

PythonPower BITime series
Read the project story +

The challenge

The dataset included many missing values. Working with seniors, I used forward fill where appropriate to prepare the data for analysis.

The approach

Before building the model, I visualized seven years of yield data in Power BI. I then developed a forecasting model that gave greater weight to harvesting time.

The result

I presented the project and my data preparation decisions as a high school graduation project. It received the session’s Best Data Science Project award.
